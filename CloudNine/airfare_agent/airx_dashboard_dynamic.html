1. Basket Construction

The representative basket comprises 24 domestic city-pairs selected using DGCA passenger-traffic data, ensuring the routes contributing to the index reflect actual travel volumes rather than an arbitrary or convenience sample.

  • DEL–BOM · DEL–BLR · BOM–BLR
  • DEL–CCU · BLR–HYD · MAA–DEL
  • … and 18 further metro and non-metro pairs

2. Advance-Purchase Windows

Fares are collected at five booking windows to capture dynamic pricing behaviour across the purchase cycle: T+1, T+7, T+15, T+30 and T+45 days before departure.

3. Route Weighting

Each route's weight in the index is derived from its share of DGCA-reported passenger traffic, so higher-volume corridors such as DEL–BOM influence APIx more than thin, low-traffic routes.

4. Index Formula

APIx(t) = Σᵢ wᵢ · Iᵢ,ₜ

where wᵢ is the traffic-derived weight of route i, and Iᵢ,ₜ is the route-level price index at time t, computed against the route's own base-period fare.

5. Data Cleaning Rules

  • Outliers beyond 3σ of the trailing route distribution are flagged and excluded from index computation
  • Sold-out and cancelled fares are dropped rather than treated as zero-price observations
  • Duplicate quotes across sources are de-duplicated by route, airline, window and timestamp
  • Base fare, taxes, UDF and convenience charges are separated so the index is built on comparable fare components

Intended Consumers

The API is designed to be consumed by statistical and monetary-policy institutions, primarily the National Statistical Office (NSO) for CPI augmentation and the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) for inflation monitoring. Response payloads are versioned and rate-limited for institutional integration.
